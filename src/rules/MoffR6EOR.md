# Section 6: Safe Havens

**6.1** Safe Havens are areas where antagonist activity is expected to be minimal, and generally non-violent. These areas are as follows:
- The arrivals planet.
- The arrivals shuttle.
- The areas on station where the arrivals shuttle docks.
- The evac shuttle after it has departed the station.
- Central Command.

**6.2** Disruptive behavior in safe havens is prohibited. This includes but is not limited to:
- Murder (both with objectives and without)
- The usage of explosives.
- The usage of lethal weapons.
- Releasing harmful chemical bombs.
- Breaking into secure areas.

**6.3** Within safe zones antagonists should lie low, not pursue their objectives, and not intentionally provoke encounters with security.

**6.4** Safe Havens are expected to remain habitable for crew throughout the course of the round, and not targets of destruction by antagonists.
- Making the evac shuttle uninhabitable before it departs is still in violation of this rule.

**6.5** Hot pursuit rule: You are allowed to finish ongoing conflicts that carry over into safe havens.
- This should still not result in an intentional breach of 6.4

**6.6** Do not take advantage of arrivals and the surrounding area to avoid antag activity
