# Section 3: Powergaming

**The rule of thumb for most powergaming rules is you should have a valid in-character justification for your actions.**
- To add, the In-Character justification should be reasonable. For example, having a character who is a gun-nut doesn't justify them carrying around a rifle every round.

**3.1** Do not powergame. Powergaming is defined as prioritizing “winning” over roleplay and reasonable character behavior.
- This rule is applied especially in the case of security and antag interactions.
- For example, if people are trying to RP a hostile scenario you shouldn't barge in guns blazing just so your side can "win" the encounter.

**3.2** While it is allowed for the crew to know vague details about antagonist functions, it is forbidden to act on that information without valid motive.
- You shouldn't act on suspicion of certain antags being present without evidence and IC reasoning.

**3.3** Do not rush for, hoard, or prepare weapons, equipment, or other items that you do not have an in-character, roleplay-friendly explanation for having.
- This rule is especially applied to members of command and security

**3.4** As a non-antagonist, don't intentionally hide antagonist objectives in unreasonable locations.
- It is considered reasonable to place it somewhere where someone looking for it could reasonably find it, such as your room (as a command member), or a member of your department.
- It is unreasonable to give it to someone who has no IC reason to have it, or stash it somewhere where nobody could reasonably find it.

**3.5** Do not exploit bugs, tricks, or unintended features to circumvent roleplay, circumvent game mechanics, or in any malicious capacity.
- For example, utilizing cryo, ghost, and death mechanics to antag roll, hiding in cryo to escape from Security, or ghosting solely to fish for antagonist roles.

**3.6** Do not intentionally breach nor metagame roleplay in order to gain unfair advantages.
- Such as, engaging someone in combat when they are busy typing.
- Fake typing to bait somebody to get closer.
- Using roleplay as an excuse to circumvent rules
