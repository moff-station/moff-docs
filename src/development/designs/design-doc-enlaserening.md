# The Enlaserening

This proposal aims to replace or compliment the current security armory with energy based weaponry, which can compete with their ballistic counterparts in a more lore-friendly manner.

## Background

In original common core lore and SS13 as a whole, Nanotrasen's weaponry was differentiated from the syndicate by their laser weapons. In place of the Mk58, you had the hybrid tazer and disabler. In place of the lecter was the laser rifle.

SS14 does away with these, introducing the lecter, drozd, kammerer and Mk58 in their place. These weapons do put a lot more combat capability into the average officer's hands, but has left energy weapons obsolete. Unless not given any other option, most players will never use an energy weapon.

The goal of this proposal is to re-introduce the disparity between Syndicate and Nanotrasen's weapon design, to make energy weapons feel more viable.

## Scope of changes

The Mk58 is removed from the security loadout, and replaced with a laser blaster. The laser blaster is effectively a pistol that fires laser bolts that do heat and stamina damage. Ideally, it is similar enough to the Mk58 to not deter security officers who are already used to the Mk58's behaviour.

The existing armory will remain as-is, but existing kinetic weapons will have descriptions modified to reflect their manufacturing origin being outside of Nanotrasen. Light arms and security equipment being manufactured by Aussec, heavy arms being manufactured by Scarborough Firearms.

Energy weapons are modified to use power cells instead of being charged in-situ. Some weapons may only fit higher grade power cells or lower grade power cells, depending on balance.

New experimental energy weapons should be added to the science armory tech tree. These weapons should be unique enough to qualify their experimental status, but practical enough to warrant their use. Effort should be taken in ensuring their sound design and effects to better match the feel of their kinetic counterparts. Weapons can be balanced by needing higher teir power cells for more intensive weapons.

A few immediate ideas:
- A high speed coilgun
- A laser sniper rifle
- An EMP ion gun

Existing energy weapons should be given a graphical and sound overhaul to bring them on par with their kinetic counterparts.

The Svalin is removed from the research pool and made into maintenance loot - with comparible rarity to the Mosin

## Game Design Rationale

The first reason for the changes is to better develop lore depth in the Nanotrasen-Syndicate relation. Having the weapons they fight with differ would be a good way to cement this, and open room for more factions to be involved in a wider universe.

The second reason for the changes is to encourage more security interaction outside of their own department. Energy weapons with applicable uses would encourage science to research them to combat specific threats, such as the ion gun to combat xenoborgs. Science will research power cell upgrades which security will need for their weapons. Energy weapons need to be manufactured with materials from cargo, encouraging more interaction with cargo for materials.

The third reason is to provide more variety in ways for security and antagonists to approach one another. Energy weapons open up a variety of new options for weapons an antagonist can get on-station, or face from security. This makes a decision for what to use against any particular threat more engaging than just picking between a shotgun or an assault rifle.

## Considerations

- Energy weapons should provide clear advantages and disadvantages compared to their kinetic counterparts, but neither should be made redundant.
- Energy weapons should be balanced around the level of involvement needed from other departments in their creation and sustainment. Security needs a base-line option for energy weaponry which works for minor threats, but also more advanced options to research for sustained, major threats such as gorlex mercs or xenoborgs.
- Power cells in place of built in magazines is an experimental change. Some weapons may need to blacklist lower grade or higher grade power cells to be appropriately balanced.
- Energy weapons introduce a new damage type for major antagonists to contend with. Armor values for antagonists such as Gorlex Mercs may need to be adjusted to suit.
